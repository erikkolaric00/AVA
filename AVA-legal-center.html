Responsible AI

Acceptable Use & AI Transparency

Effective: [DATE]

1. Required AI disclosure

Clients must ensure that people are clearly informed, at the latest at first interaction, that they are communicating with an AI system unless this is already obvious. The notice must be understandable and accessible.

Suggested call opening: “Hello, I’m AVA, the AI assistant for [Business]. I can help with bookings and general questions. You can ask for a staff member at any time.”

2. Permitted purpose

AVA may be used for ordinary business administration, such as answering general business questions, handling bookings, routing inquiries, and preparing operational summaries, with reasonable human oversight.

3. Prohibited use

  • Emergency dispatch, crisis response, or any situation where delay or error may threaten life, health, safety, or fundamental rights.
  • Medical diagnosis or advice; legal, financial, credit, insurance, employment, education, migration, law-enforcement or other high-impact decisions.
  • Unlawful discrimination, biometric categorisation, emotion inference, social scoring, manipulation, exploitation of vulnerability, or deceptive impersonation.
  • Harassment, threats, illegal content, fraud, spam, malware, credential theft, unlawful surveillance, or infringement of privacy or intellectual-property rights.
  • Collecting sensitive or children’s data without written approval, a valid legal basis and appropriate safeguards.
  • Removing or hiding required AI notices, misrepresenting AI output as guaranteed fact, or relying on it without human review where an error could materially harm someone.

4. Human escalation

Clients must provide a reasonable path to human assistance. AVA should escalate complaints, uncertainty, special requests, suspected emergencies, and matters outside its configured scope. AVA must not pretend that escalation occurred if it did not.

5. Enforcement

We may investigate reported misuse and limit or suspend affected functions. Where appropriate, we will notify the client and allow remediation. Serious unlawful use may be reported to competent authorities where legally required.
