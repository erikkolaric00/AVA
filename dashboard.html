Guest information

Opening hours

Mon–Thu 11:00–22:00 · Fri–Sat 11:00–23:00 · Sun 11:00–21:00. Kitchen closes 30 minutes before closing.

Dietary options

Vegetarian and vegan dishes are available. Gluten-free preparation is possible for selected dishes, but the kitchen is not a certified allergen-free environment.

Accessibility

Main dining room and restroom are wheelchair accessible. Ask staff to reserve an accessible table.

Children

High chairs are available free of charge. Children’s portions are available for selected meals.

Parking

Free guest parking is available behind the restaurant with 14 spaces. Street parking is available nearby.

Takeaway

Takeaway orders are accepted during kitchen hours. Typical pickup time is 20–35 minutes depending on demand.

Cancellation

Standard reservations can be cancelled at any time. Please notify the restaurant as early as possible.

Terrace

Terrace seating is weather-dependent and cannot be guaranteed during rain or strong wind.
